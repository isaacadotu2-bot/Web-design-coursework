About Us

Chromelite Technologies Limited is a Kampala-based company providing branding, signage and structural steel supply. We have served businesses, institutions and property developers across the Central Region since our founding.

Our team combines design skills with fabrication expertise, so a client can move from a logo concept to a finished sign or steel structure under one roof.

Our Services

We offer a complete range of visual identity and fabrication services, tailored to the needs of small businesses and large projects alike.

Every job begins with a site or brand assessment, followed by design approval and a clear delivery timeline before production starts.

Signage

Illuminated and non-illuminated signs for shops, offices and estates.

Vehicle Branding

Full wraps, partial livery and decals for fleets, vans and taxis.

Printing

Banners, posters, brochures and large-format print for campaigns and events.

Steel Fabrication

Gates, frames, sign structures and custom metalwork cut and welded to specification.

Our Projects

Over the years we have delivered branding packages for retail outlets, fleet livery for transport operators and steel structures for commercial buildings.

Each project is documented from design to installation, so clients can see the result and the process behind it. Samples are available on request.
